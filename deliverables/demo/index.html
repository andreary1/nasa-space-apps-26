About the data

Catalog and parameters inherited from main. Illustrative records and presets are not validated measurements. Available references are lunar craters and Martian dunes; combinations without a reference receive no score.

The engine uses weighted Euclidean distance, cosine similarity and Bagnold scaling for dunes, with the original weights and coefficients.
